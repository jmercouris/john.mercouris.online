How can we make eye contact over the web?
=========================================

When collaborating with individuals across the globe, we lose a part
of our humanity. We cease to be people, we become pictures on a
screen. In order to rehumanize our interactions we can take a few
steps. One of these is to make sure that we have a high fidelity
webcam and microphone. Not only does this help other individuals
recognize that we're humans, it also helps us appear more credible. [1]_

Additionally, as part of rehumanizing ourselves, we might think about
how we can bring back one of the most important parts of human
conversation; eye contact. Apple has spent a lot of effort in trying
to do this via `FaceTime Eye Contact`. Unfortunately, this
implementation was problematic, and nothing quite works like a
hardware solution. To this end, I designed a foldable telescoping
webcam mount.

Firstly, because my computer monitor is on a monitor arm, I decided to
reuse the VESA interface. Using standoffs, I mounted my webcam mount
through the same screw holes that mount to the VESA interface.

It can still be used from the stowed position, it just is not at the
most optimal angle. For quick impromptu calls though, it can be
sufficient. Because my monitor is on an arm, I can also move the
monitor to slightly improve the angle. However, if I want to _really_
focus on the conversation, I can fold it out.

|IMG_4074|

|IMG_4075|

Using this interface, I can line things up perfectly with my eyes. The
individual I am conversing with is in the center of my screen, and I
am "looking" at them. You'll also observe the presence of a fill
light. I can use this to eliminate shadows on the image captured by
the webcam.

All in, I am very satisfied with this approach. The angle is perfect,
and the audio quality is impeccable.

can download the FreeCAD design files:
